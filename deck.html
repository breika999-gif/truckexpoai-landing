Problem

Commercial driving is complex, dangerous, and heavily penalized.

  • Regulatory penalties. EU Regulation 561/2006 (hours-of-service) fines reach up to €5,000 per violation, and the Smart Tachograph V2 mandate is forcing retrofits across the fleet right now.
  • Unsafe routing. Consumer apps (Google Maps, Waze) don't know truck dimensions — they route heavy trucks under low bridges and into weight-restricted zones.
  • Driver distraction. Tapping through navigation and watching tachograph timers while driving is a top safety hazard — and Europe is short ~500,000 drivers.
Solution

One voice. Hands on the wheel.

  • Gemini voice co-pilot. Ask anything by voice — routes, parking, rest times, restrictions — and get spoken answers plus live map actions. No screen tapping.
  • Truck-safe navigation. HGV routing by vehicle profile (weight, height, length, axles, ADR hazmat) with live restrictions, driving bans by country, speed cameras and parking.
  • Automatic tachograph / HOS. Tracks EU 561/2006 driving and rest limits, warns before the 4.5h / 9h / weekly limits, and finds legal parking before time runs out.
Why Gemini

Built on Gemini, not bolted on.

  • In production today. Gemini 2.0 Flash powers the in-cab assistant and voice transcription — not a roadmap promise, it's live in the app.
  • Function-calling navigation. Voice intent → structured map actions (set route, add waypoint, avoid restriction, find parking) executed on the map.
  • Multilingual by design. EU drivers cross borders daily (Bulgarian, Polish, Romanian, Spanish…). Gemini's language range is a real edge over legacy nav.
  • Where we're going. Gemini-only navigation, Gemini Vision ("snap the road sign, I'll tell you if you can pass"), and Gemini Live for real-time in-cab conversation.

Status & Traction

Business Model

Two revenue lines.

  • B2C subscription. Monthly plan for individual drivers — premium voice, HOS planning, and navigation.
  • B2B fleet. Dashboard + API licenses for fleet operators to track real-time driver compliance and telemetry — where the recurring revenue scales.
