Navigation

Truck route control

AI cockpit

Two assistants, voice and map actions

  • GPT-4o map actions for routes, waypoints, POIs and app intents
  • Gemini driver assistant with route, tacho, weather and memory context
  • Voice recording -> transcription -> Gemini/GPT map action
  • Route-aware answers using actual distance, duration and remaining drive time
Tacho

EU driving-time logic

  • EU 561/2006 continuous, daily and weekly drive-time logic
  • 30/20/10 minute break warnings with parking search trigger
  • Bluetooth tachograph live context when connected, local fallback when not
  • Rest logging, offline session queue and end-of-day shift summary
Parking & search

TransParking, fuel and Google fallback

  • 59,436 TransParking truck parkings from local cache
  • Truck parking and fuel POIs near driver or along active route
  • TomTom search with Google Places fallback and a G badge
  • Business search results, saved POIs and TransParking handoff
Safety

Restrictions, bans and cameras

  • Overpass truck restrictions for height, width, weight, no-truck and ADR
  • Truck ban calendar from trafficban.com for selected date
  • Speed camera warnings along route via Overpass API
  • Mapbox Tilequery tunnel/bridge checks plus TomTom traffic alerts
Backend & fleet

APIs behind the cockpit

  • Flask APIs for route calculation, POIs, tacho, cameras and AI

  • Android MVP validated through private road testing
