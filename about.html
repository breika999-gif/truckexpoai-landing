First-hand problem ownership

Miroslav is an active European long-haul truck driver building the product around problems encountered in the cab: truck-safe routing, legal driving time, reliable parking, traffic context and hands-free interaction. Product decisions are tested against real driving workflows rather than a generic passenger-car navigation model.

TruckExpoAI is currently a solo-founder, pre-revenue technical alpha. The immediate goal is rigorous road validation and the formation of engineering and data partnerships that can turn the working prototype into a dependable commercial product.
